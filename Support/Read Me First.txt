Icecast Control: install

1. Drag "Icecast Control" onto the Applications folder.

2. Open it from Applications. The first time, macOS will say it can't check the app
   because it isn't from the App Store:
     • Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
     • Scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to "Icecast Control", then confirm.
   (You only need to do this once.)

3. A welcome guide walks you through the rest: your stream server, internet access,
   and what audio to stream.

Look for the antenna icon in the menu bar at the top of the screen.

Streaming from OBS? Also download and install the separate OBS plugin
(Icecast-Control-OBS-Plugin.zip) from the same download page.
